# Retail Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses a sample retail dataset to evaluate sales performance, profitability, product and category performance, regional results, customer segments, and discount practices.

The goal is to turn raw sales data into useful business insights and recommendations through Excel analysis and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

**Dataset:** Sample Superstore  
**Analysis period:** 2014–2017  
**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Power BI, DAX

## Business Questions
1. What are the overall sales and profitability results?
2. How do sales and profit change over time?
3. Which product categories and sub-categories perform best and worst?
4. Which regions and customer segments contribute most to performance?
5. How are discounts associated with profitability?
6. What actions could help improve business performance?

## Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|

| Total Profit | $286,397.02 |
| Total Orders | 5,009 |
| Profit Margin | 12.47% |

## Key Findings
- **Technology** generated the highest category sales, at approximately $836,154.
- **The West region** recorded the highest sales, at approximately $725,458.
- The **Consumer segment** contributed the highest sales and profit among customer segments.
- The **Tables** sub-category recorded a loss of approximately $17,725.
- Higher discount levels were associated with weaker profitability. The 50% and 70% discount levels recorded negative total profit.
- Sales and profit reached their highest annual totals in 2017 within the dataset.

## Tools and Methods
### Microsoft Excel
- Data quality checks and formatting
- Date and discount-group preparation
- PivotTable-based analysis
- KPI calculations and performance comparisons

### Power BI
- Interactive KPI cards and charts
- Date table and year-based analysis
- DAX measures for sales, profit, and year-over-year changes
- Slicers for year, region, segment, and category
- Metric selectors for comparing sales and profit

## Recommendations
- Review high-discount transactions and introduce appropriate discount approval controls.
- Investigate the losses in the Tables sub-category.
- Monitor profit margin alongside sales when evaluating performance.
- Review product, region, and customer-segment performance to identify opportunities for improvement.
- Test discount strategies carefully before wider implementation.

## Dataset Limitations
The Superstore dataset is a sample retail dataset representing US-based operations, not verified records from a real company. The analysis uses the supplied profit values because a separate cost column is not available. Relationships between discount levels and profitability should not be interpreted as proof that discounts alone caused losses.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Excel and Power BI can be used to assess retail performance, identify profitability concerns, and communicate findings through business-focused analysis and interactive reporting.
